Whisker Wars

Prototype · Level 1, The Village Green · Topple both wizard towers

The wizards want your cats

Across the stream, two wizard towers are recruiting apprentices to collect the cats of Mothwick as familiars. You have about three minutes before the first raid.

  • Drag a cat to pick it up. Drop it on a Dojo (Scrapper), a Workshop (Tinker) or a bed (Tabby). Or click a cat and press 1–4.Or tap a cat and pick a job at the bottom.
  • ClickTap a bed to pull a cat out. Tabbies in beds breed kittens. Double-clickDouble-tap a bed to upgrade it.
  • Watch for glowing runes. A wizard's spell lands where the rune is. Drag a cat out of it to dodge. Hitting a wizard mid-cast can make it fizzle.
  • Cats working alone get scared of wizards. Scrappers protect them, fight better in packs, and rank up as they win.
  • Drag empty ground or use WASD to look around. Scroll or pinch to zoom.
  • Drag empty ground to look around. Pinch with two fingers to zoom. Orders opens research and settings.

Turn your device sideways for a bigger battlefield.
